# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful space for everyone. Participants are expected to communicate constructively and to respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Expected behavior

- Be courteous and professional.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions when needed.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, sexualized content, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the repository's security reporting channel or by contacting a maintainer. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
